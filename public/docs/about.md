# About

cartesiancs ( pronounced `/kɑːrˈtiːʒəns/`)

We embrace open source culture and work to **reduce reliance on centralized authority** as much as possible.

One of the projects we worked on is [cartesiancs/cartcut](https://github.com/cartesiancs/cartcut), and our current ongoing project is [cartesiancs/vessel](https://github.com/cartesiancs/vessel), [cartesiancs/map3d](https://github.com/cartesiancs/map3d). [and more.](https://github.com/cartesiancs)

Our ultimate goal is to empower individuals to live **independently** to give each person the strength to change the world and the freedom to pursue their own dreams. That is our mission.

As part of that journey, we chose open source as our means of achieving it.

### Origin

The name _"cartesiancs"_ comes from the Cartesian Coordinate System. It was Descartes who united geometry and algebra, opening a new paradigm in mathematics and heralding the dawn of the Renaissance.

The Cartesian plane is infinite. We hope that on this plane, everyone will sketch their own ideas, analyze the world, and reason through it. Just as the simplest combinations give rise to emergent complexity, we believe open source is how technological freedom becomes available to all.

If you'd like to learn more about our brand story, visit our blog at https://blog.cartesiancs.com/

### Vision

The project we are currently focused on is **[landlink](https://landlink.sh/)**. The map3d, cartcut, and vessel projects are all being integrated into landlink. landlink is designed to let individuals build their own communication networks, and it operates over LoRa networks. Going further, integration with drones will enable autonomous flight on top of the landlink platform, such as setting waypoint based flight paths or surveying specific areas.

The vision portrayed in _<Star Trek>_ is the same as our goal. "to send humanity far beyond into space", and to prove that a single person can survive there indefinitely. And we believe that independent survival for the individual is possible not only in space but here on Earth as well. Achieving this requires the self sustaining ground communication network that landlink implements.

Ultimately, even if Earth becomes an uninhabitable environment, or an extremely harsh one, our vision is to give humanity the minimal set of tools it needs to survive and endure. That is our vision.

And this runs on fundamentally the same technology as survival in space. Space is harsh. For humanity to pursue its own dreams, people should not merely survive atop massive infrastructure funded at the scale of nation states. Instead, each individual should be able to source their own supplies and build their own ventures. Yet this is a domain that demands advanced technology, much like calculating the orbit of a spacecraft. To make it possible, we are developing AI that reduces the need for human intervention and lets the system operate on its own.
