# CartCut tutorial

CartCut is a free, open-source video editor for macOS. There is no subscription, no watermark and no account. This tutorial takes you from installing the app to exporting your first video, and then walks through every feature one page at a time.

![The CartCut editor with a short project open](img/editor-overview.webp "A project with three video clips, a title and background music.")

## What you can make

- **Cut edits.** Put clips on a timeline, split them, trim them and rearrange them.
- **Titles and captions.** Type text in 20 bundled fonts, or let CartCut transcribe speech into captions for you.
- **Motion.** Animate position, size, rotation and opacity with keyframes, or apply one of 19 ready-made animations.
- **Color.** Grade with 15 sliders, 80 built-in LUTs and 17 blend modes.
- **Effects and transitions.** 39 effects and 37 transitions, plus chroma key and blur.
- **Screen recordings.** Record your screen and let CartCut add smooth zooms where you clicked.
- **AI editing.** Connect Claude Code and ask for edits in plain language.

> [!NOTE]
> This tutorial is written for CartCut 0.5.10 on macOS. Keyboard shortcuts use <kbd>⌘</kbd> (Command). Some labels in the app are only available in English, so the Korean interface shows a mix of Korean and English.

## Getting help

- Search this tutorial with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>.
- Inside the app, open **Help ▸ Show Tutorial** for a short guided tour of the basics.
- Report a bug or request a feature on [GitHub](https://github.com/cartesiancs/cartcut/issues).
